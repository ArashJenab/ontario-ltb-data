What period does it cover?

Orders the Board issued from 2 January to 30 June 2026: 180 days, 49,269 orders on 44,743 cases. Not a full year, so annual figures are scaled up and say so. No earlier period is published, so no trend can be measured yet.

How current is it?

Retrieved 1 October 2026. The newest order in the file is dated 30 June, 93 days earlier. That gap is the Board's: it adds orders to its catalogue in batches, about three months after they issue. On 13 August the newest was dated 29 May.

Is it straight from the Board?

Yes. Every count is taken from the Board's own LTB Order Catalogue on data.ontario.ca, through its public API. The catalogue does not carry amounts, rents, attendance or outcomes, so those are read from 8,125 of the order documents it links to. Rates use the 2021 Census.
